# Product

## Register

product

## Users

University students who use an Android phone to track runs, daily activity, meals, fitness goals, and campus challenges. They need fast, readable status updates while moving and straightforward controls for logging or reviewing progress.

## Product Purpose

UniMove combines activity tracking, nutrition logging, campus challenges, leaderboards, and community features in one mobile fitness app. Success means students can understand their current progress quickly and keep a reliable record of their activity.

## Brand Personality

Active, approachable, dependable. The interface should feel focused and encouraging without becoming noisy or decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic sample-app styling, decorative card stacks, weak gray contrast, saturated inactive states, excessive animation, and loading states that shift important content or appear disconnected from the area being loaded.

## Design Principles

1. Show current progress clearly before secondary detail.
2. Keep common tracking and logging actions easy to reach.
3. Use color and motion to communicate state, not decoration.
4. Preserve user trust with visible loading, error, empty, and sync states.
5. Keep interaction patterns consistent across fitness, nutrition, and community screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, clear labels, touch targets suitable for mobile use, layouts that adapt to common Android screen sizes, and reduced-motion-safe transitions.
