# Product

## Register

product

## Users

UniMove wearable users who start, monitor, pause, stop, and review a run directly on a small Wear OS watch. They may be moving, sweating, and glancing at the display briefly, so the interface must prioritize large metrics, clear states, and reliable one-tap controls.

## Product Purpose

UniMove Wearable tracks a running session on the watch using Health Services, sends live and final session data to the paired phone over the Wear OS Data Layer, and preserves the last run locally when the phone is unavailable.

## Brand Personality

Focused, athletic, dependable. The watch UI should feel like a compact training instrument rather than a generic sample app.

## Anti-references

Avoid phone-sized layouts squeezed onto the watch, decorative card stacks, weak gray contrast, tiny controls, saturated inactive states, and UI changes that obscure sync or tracking status.

## Design Principles

1. Glance first: the most important number on each screen must be readable in under one second.
2. Preserve trust: sync, permission, support, and error states must be visible without changing the tracking pipeline.
3. Round-screen native: every screen must fit a 40mm round watch with safe horizontal insets and vertical scrolling.
4. Active sessions are persistent: while a run is active, the app should remain visible or easy to return to after ambient/sleep transitions.
5. State over decoration: color and motion communicate running, paused, syncing, error, or summary states only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high contrast on black backgrounds, large tap targets for primary actions, concise labels, scrollable content for small screens, and reduced visual complexity in ambient mode to protect readability and battery.
